Chennai Students Bring New Life to Community Gardens

A student-led campaign encourages residents to plant trees and look after their neighbourhood.

Written by Priya Kumar | Published on 22 November 2026

This is a fictional story for a student project.

A Greener Neighbourhood

A group of college students in Chennai has started a campaign to improve local community gardens. The students are inviting residents to join them in planting trees and cleaning shared spaces.

The idea began when the students noticed that a nearby garden needed regular care. They decided to organise a weekend activity and ask neighbours to help.

Small Steps, Lasting Change

The team plans to plant native trees and explain how to care for them. Volunteers will also remove litter and make sure the young plants receive enough water.

“We wanted to do something useful for our area. Even a few hours each week can make a difference.”

These were the words of Ananya Ravi, the student coordinating the campaign.

How You Can Help

You do not need gardening experience to take part. Residents can help in several ways:

  • Join a weekend planting session.
  • Help water the young trees.
  • Donate spare gardening tools.
  • Encourage friends and neighbours to join.

Campaign Schedule

Planned activities for the community campaign
Location Activity Day
Community Garden Planting saplings Saturday
Neighbourhood Park Watering and cleaning Sunday

What Happens Next?

The students will review the garden after one month to check how the plants are growing. They hope more residents will join and help keep the campaign going.

The team believes that working together can make shared spaces cleaner, greener and more welcoming.
